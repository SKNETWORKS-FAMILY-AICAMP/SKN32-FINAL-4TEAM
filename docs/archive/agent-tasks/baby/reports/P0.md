### What changed structurally (SR01–SR09 against the fresh-database policy)

`db/migrations/0012_schema_reduction_destructive.sql` is the actual destructive completion the prior
two reports deferred. It runs SR03 fresh-install precondition guards first (checking that every
column staged by 0010/0011 already mirrors its legacy source rows — see the destructive rehearsal
above for a real failing case), then:

- Drops `notification` and `dataset` schemas entirely (3+4 tables), `shared` schema (after rebinding
  every surviving `updated_at` trigger from `shared.set_updated_at()` to a new `app.set_updated_at()`),
  `config.domain_version`, `identity.user_preference`, `catalog.product_category_membership`,
  `planning.plan_node`/`owned_item`/`purchase_line`/`fulfillment_allocation`,
  `assets.material_revision`/`material_applicability`, `evidence.source`,
  `engine.candidate_evidence`/`validation_target`/`validation_evidence`,
  `community.review_revision`/`pc_build`/`pc_build_version`/`pc_build_component`, and the now-unused
  `config.schema_reduction_mapping`.
- Merges `community.review`+`review_revision`+`pc_build`+`pc_build_version` into two tables
  (`community.review` with `record_type IN ('review','build')`, `community.review_component`),
  documented in schema-v1.md as required by the P0 work order.
- Validates the three `NOT VALID` constraints 0011 added (`recommendation_candidate_evidence_refs_v1_check`,
  `validation_result_issues_array_check`, `requirement_slot_key_nonempty_check`) against real table
  contents, not just declares them.
- Live SQL consumers were converted in the same change, not left pointing at dropped tables:
  `db/seed.py` (no more `shared.unit`/`config.domain_version` writes), `db/seed_catalog.py`/
  `src/repo/product_repo.py` (`catalog.offer_observation.source_name/source_type` replace the
  `evidence.source` FK), `src/repo/plan_repo.py` (`ensure_requirement` manages `planning.requirement`
  directly by `slot_key`, no `plan_node`), `src/repo/engine_repo.py` (`link_validation_target`/
  `link_validation_evidence` write into `validation_result.issues` jsonb, same call signatures used
  by `src/services/recommendation_service.py`), `src/repo/rag_repo.py` (full rewrite of the `ELIGIBLE`
  query and `publish_manual`/`revoke_material` to read/write `assets.product_material` directly
  instead of joining `material_revision`/`material_applicability`, and `evidence.evidence.source_name/
  source_type` instead of `evidence.source`), `scripts/rag_manual.py` (`create_test_run` no longer
  creates a `config.domain_version` row).
- `src/repo/notification_repo.py`, `src/repo/dataset_repo.py`, `src/services/notification_service.py`,
  `src/workers/notification_worker.py` were deleted — grepped first to confirm nothing imported them
  (they were pure `NotImplementedError` stubs over now-dropped schemas). No `/lists/{id}/alert` route
